## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-touch, executive real estate and asset management atmosphere tailored specifically for high-velocity mobile management. It synthesizes **Modern Luxury Minimalism** with **Tactile Glass Finishes**, eliminating administrative clutter in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, decisive status badges, and luminous financial metrics.

### Persona & Tone
- **Prestigious & Composed:** Grounded by deep slate canvases and illuminated with jewel emerald and champagne tones, the interface evokes the privacy and control of a private wealth dashboard.
- **Operational Clarity:** Complex property data—partitions, studio layouts, utility splits (ADDC, WiFi, maintenance), and lease turnaround timers—are compressed into bite-sized, scannable visual units without cognitive fatigue.
- **Tactile Polish:** Surfaces utilize subtle inner lighting, satin-grade borders, and smooth curvature, offering an immediate sense of craft and responsiveness to touch.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Base Style:** Curated modern minimalism accented with soft optical blurs and warm metallic highlights.
- **Micro-Atmosphere:** High-contrast structural cards set against layered neutral backgrounds, pairing architectural precision with warm residential luxury.

## Colors

The palette leverages an architectural deep slate environment to allow deep emeralds and metallic accents to project value, status, and financial clarity.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0D8259` - Rich Emerald):** The foundational anchor of financial health, collected payments, high occupancy, and positive asset performance. Used for prime interaction buttons, positive ROI readouts, and primary active states.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for luxury asset tiers, exclusive studio/partition labels, verified tenant credentials, and key annualized yield summaries.
- **Tertiary (`#E57A44` - Soft Warning Amber):** Applied selectively to urgent transitional states: 'Leaving Soon' tenancies, pending lease signature drops, and overdue utility reconciliations.
- **Neutral (`#0F141C` - Deep Slate Obsidian):** The foundational screen background. Supplemented by structured dark neutrals:
  - Surface Tier 1 (Canvas): `#0F141C`
  - Surface Tier 2 (Card Background): `#18202C`
  - Surface Tier 3 (Inner Well / Input Fill): `#212B3B`
  - Surface Border (Hairline Outlines): `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

### Status Badges & Utilities
- **Occupied:** Background `rgba(13, 130, 89, 0.16)`, Text `#34D399`, Border `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.28)`.
- **Vacant:** Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.14)`, Text `#FB7185`, Border `rgba(251, 113, 133, 0.25)`.
- **Leaving Soon:** Background `rgba(229, 122, 68, 0.16)`, Text `#FDBA74`, Border `rgba(253, 186, 116, 0.28)`.
- **Utility Indicators (ADDC / Water & Power):** Light Sky `#38BDF8`.
- **Utility Indicators (WiFi & High Speed):** Electric Violet `#A78BFA`.
- **Utility Indicators (Maintenance & Chiller):** Champagne `#E9D58F`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes architectural elegance with high-speed computational data inspection.

### Type Pairings
- **Display & Section Titles (`Syne`):** Imparts distinct editorial sophistication, sculptural proportions, and high-end residential character.
- **Interface & Operational Copy (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean optical balance, high legibility on dark glass backgrounds, and rapid comprehension for dense mobile property feeds.
- **Numbers, Metrics & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Fixed-width energy engineered for currency metrics (AED/USD), room unit identifiers (e.g., #402-B Partition), dates, and status chips.

### Scaling & Legibility Rules
- Never drop mobile body text below 12px.
- Use uppercase for all micro-labels (`label-status` and `label-meta`) with subtle positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to guarantee legibility across compressed tag chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built entirely on a mobile-first fluid model optimized for natural one-handed thumb interaction (safe-zone bottom ergonomics).

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Grid Strategy:** 4-column fluid layout on phone displays (360px - 480px width) scaling into 8-column card spreads on small tablets (481px - 768px).
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Canvas padding uses `margin-mobile` (16px) with an intentional vertical padding reservation of 88px at screen bottoms to clear persistent bottom floating navigation and action triggers.
- **Touch Reach Density:** All key decision controls (filter tags, card action triggers, rent receipt download taps) are bound to a minimum touch bounding box of 44x44px, spaced by a minimum of `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous tonal layering and precise ambient lighting rather than heavy generic dropshadows.

### Layering Blueprint
- **Base Plane (Level 0 - `#0F141C`):** Non-elevated screen floor.
- **Card Tier (Level 1 - `#18202C`):** Framed with a 1px composite outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and illuminated by an ambient drop shadow: `0px 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.40)`.
- **Interactive Sheets & Modals (Level 2 - `#1F2B3B`):** Features a subtle top rim-light (`1px inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and an elevation blur: `0px 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Floating Floating Action Elements (Level 3):** Glassmorphic fill (`rgba(24, 32, 44, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with emerald or gold inner-glow halos (`0px 0px 24px rgba(13, 130, 89, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, organic roundness configured to produce sleek cards and pill-shaped badges across all modules.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Structural Cards & Sheets (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem):** High-level housing for apartment listings, utility breakdowns, and tenant overviews. Softens technical data and feels tactile on handheld screens.
- **Action Triggers & Inputs (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Used for primary conversion buttons, search inputs, and payment filter rows.
- **Badges & Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status chips ('Occupied', 'Vacant', 'Leaving Soon'), studio layout tags, and utility meter counts.
